# Presentation design system

## Start with communication, not decoration

Choose a design that supports the setting and content. A dense operating review benefits from restrained color and compact information design. A keynote or product reveal can use fewer elements, larger type, and stronger contrast. Preserve an existing coherent brand unless the user asks to replace it.

## Define global tokens before pages

Use `update_ppt_design` to establish:

- background and foreground contrast;
- one title family and one body family;
- a small accent palette with clear roles;
- page size;
- footer and numbering policy;
- a restrained shared transition.

Use a preset as a coherent base, then override only deliberate brand requirements. Avoid assigning a different decorative color to every page.

## Typography

- Make takeaway titles the strongest element.
- Use body text for evidence, implications, and labels, not essay paragraphs.
- Keep title and body families consistent across the deck.
- Use weight, size, and whitespace before introducing more colors.
- Prefer shorter lines and fewer bullets over shrinking text to fit.

## Color

- Maintain strong foreground/background contrast.
- Give the primary accent a stable semantic role, such as the recommendation or current state.
- Reserve warning and negative colors for actual warnings and negative values.
- Recolor editable shapes, tables, and charts during a global restyle; preserve photographic media.
- Inspect data-heavy and image-heavy pages after changing a theme because they expose contrast problems quickly.

## Layout rhythm

- Reuse a small family of page archetypes rather than one identical template or a different layout on every page.
- Keep stable margins and title positions unless a deliberate hero page breaks the rhythm.
- Use alignment and whitespace to express grouping.
- Prefer one dominant visual region and one supporting region over many equal boxes.

## Motion

Use a single subtle transition for most professional decks. Add element animation only when order or causality matters. Motion must explain sequence, not decorate static information.
