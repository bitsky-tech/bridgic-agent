# Presentation quality check

## Story

- Do the titles alone form a coherent argument?
- Does every page have one clear role and takeaway?
- Are important claims supported by visible evidence?
- Are there repeated pages, missing transitions in reasoning, or an unclear final ask?

## Page composition

- Is the takeaway visible before supporting detail?
- Is anything present only to fill space?
- Are related objects grouped through alignment, spacing, and color?
- Is the page too dense compared with its neighbors?
- Would splitting or simplifying be better than shrinking text?

## Visual system

- Are title/body typography, backgrounds, accent roles, margins, and footer treatment consistent?
- Is contrast sufficient on every background?
- Do charts and tables use the same semantic colors?
- Are transitions restrained and consistent?

## Content integrity

- Are terminology, dates, units, decimals, totals, and labels consistent?
- Are image and chart sources appropriate and legible?
- Are notes, hyperlinks, and media preserved where required?
- Did any edit accidentally drop stable ids or supported frontmatter?

## Live verification

Inspect representative pages in the editor: cover, densest text page, image-heavy page, table/chart page, and closing page. After a global design change, inspect at least one light/dark edge case and one data-heavy page. Repair only pages with observed problems.
